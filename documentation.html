PART 1: INTRODUCTION TO BIZMATE

1.1 What is BizMate?

BizMate is a comprehensive, offline-capable business management system designed specifically for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Uganda and across Africa. Unlike cloud-based solutions, BizMate stores all data locally on the user's device, ensuring complete privacy, offline accessibility, and zero recurring costs for basic features.

🎯 Core Purpose

To provide affordable, accessible business management tools that help African SMEs formalize operations, reduce losses, and increase profitability.

Key Philosophy

Privacy-first architecture where your data stays on your device. No cloud, no servers, no third-party access.

Target Market

Retail shops, restaurants, wholesalers, service providers, and any business needing simple financial tracking.

1.2 Key Features at a Glance

Module Features
User Management 3 role levels (Admin, Manager, Staff), user approval, permissions management
Sales Product/service sales, cart management, receipts, credit tracking, payment recording
Purchases Supplier management, multi-item purchases, payment tracking, debt management
Expenses Daily expense tracking, categorization, history viewing
Inventory Automatic stock updates, low stock alerts, valuation reports
Staff Employee records, salary payments, payment history
Income Non-sales income tracking (rental, interest, etc.)
Reports Profit/loss analysis, item-level profitability, visual charts
Export PDF reports, Excel spreadsheets for all modules

1.3 System Architecture

BizMate uses a pure client-side architecture with no server component:

Architecture Layers:
  • Presentation Layer: HTML/CSS - User interface and styling
  • Logic Layer: JavaScript - Application functionality and business rules
  • Data Layer: localStorage - Browser-based data persistence
Important Note: All data is stored locally on your device. Clearing browser data will permanently delete all BizMate information. Always export important data as backup.

Page URL: forgot password.html

  1. Click "Forgot Password?" on login page

  3. System generates 6-digit code (visible in console for demo)

  5. Enter and confirm new password
  6. Click "Reset Password"
  7. Redirected to login

4.3 Cart Management

Adding Items:

  • For products: Select from dropdown, adjust quantity, click "Add Item"
  • For services: Enter name and price, click "Add Item"
  • System checks stock availability for products
  • Running total updates automatically

Cart Table Columns:

Column Description
Item Product or service name
Qty Quantity
Price Unit price
Total Qty × Price
Action Remove button (🗑️)

4.4 Payment Methods

Method Description Use Case
Cash Physical currency In-person payments
Mobile Money MTN/Airtel Money Digital payments
Bank Bank transfer Large transactions
Credit Customer owes balance Trusted customers

Credit Sales:

  • Enter amount paid (less than total)
  • Balance shows in red
  • Customer becomes debtor
  • Can record additional payments later

15.2 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is BizMate really free?

A: Yes! The Free plan includes all basic features forever. Premium features are available through affordable subscriptions.

Q: Do I need internet to use BizMate?

A: No. BizMate works completely offline after the initial page load.

Q: Where is my data stored?

A: All data is stored in your browser's localStorage on your device. No data is sent to any server.

Q: What happens if I clear my browser data?

A: All BizMate data will be permanently deleted. Always export important data first.

Q: How do I backup my data?

A: Use the export features (PDF/Excel) on history pages to save copies of your data.

Q: What browsers are supported?

A: Chrome 60+, Firefox 55+, Safari 11+, Edge 79+. Internet Explorer is not supported.
