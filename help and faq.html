Getting Started

Welcome to Optra!

Optra is a comprehensive business management solution designed to help you track sales, manage inventory, process staff payments, and analyze your business performance - all with a privacy-first approach where your data stays on your device.

Quick Start Guide

  • Create Your Account: Go to the registration page and fill in your details. The first person to register with a Business ID becomes the Admin.
  • Set Up Your Business: After logging in, go to any page and enter your business information.
  • Add Products: Use the "Add Purchases" page to record items you buy.
  • Start Selling: Go to "New Sales" to record customer purchases.
  • Track Expenses: Record your business expenses to see complete profit/loss analysis.

Account Management

Registration & Login

  • Registration: Fill in your details including Business ID. The first user with a Business ID becomes Admin automatically.
  • Login: Use your email/username and password.
  • Password Reset: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
  • Delete Account: You can permanently delete your account from the Profile page.

Payments

How to Pay

  • Select a Plan: Go to the Subscription page and choose your preferred plan.
  • Choose Network: Select either MTN or Airtel.
  • Enter Phone Number: Enter your mobile money number.
  • Confirm Payment: Check your phone for the payment prompt and enter PIN.

Supported Networks

  • MTN Uganda: 077, 078, 079 prefixes
  • Airtel Uganda: 070, 074, 075 prefixes

Troubleshooting

Can't Log In?

  • Check your email/username and password
  • Use "Forgot Password" to reset

Data Not Saving?

  • Optra uses localStorage - data is stored in your browser
  • Clearing browser data will delete Optra information
  • Always export important data as backup

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I enter a Business ID that already exists?

You can still register, but you must wait for the admin to approve your access before you can log in.

Who becomes the admin?

The first person to register a unique Business ID is automatically considered the admin for that business.

How can I change my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" feature on the login page, or go to Settings → Change Password.

How do I subscribe for full access?

Go to Settings → Subscription and choose your preferred plan. Pay using MTN or Airtel mobile money.

Can I use Optra on my phone?

Yes! Optra is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely! All your data is stored locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers.
