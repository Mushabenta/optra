About the App

What is Optra?

Optra is a comprehensive business management system designed specifically for small and medium enterprises in Uganda and across Africa. It helps business owners track sales, manage inventory, process staff salaries, and analyze profits.

Our Mission

To democratize business management technology by providing affordable, accessible, and powerful tools that help African SMEs formalize their operations, reduce losses, and increase profitability.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted business management platform for over 1 million SMEs across Africa by 2030, empowering entrepreneurs with digital tools that drive growth and financial inclusion.

Key Features

Sales Management

Process sales for products and services, generate receipts automatically, track payments and balances.

Inventory Control

Track stock levels in real-time, low stock alerts, manage purchase orders, auto-update inventory.

Profit Analysis

Calculate profits by comparing buying vs selling prices. Visual charts show revenue and profit margins.

Staff Management

Manage employee records, track attendance, process salary payments with complete history.

Expense Tracking

Record and categorize business expenses, track spending patterns, see impact on profitability.

Reports & Export

Generate professional PDF reports and Excel spreadsheets for sales, inventory, expenses, profit.

Business Modules

Point of Sale

  • Product & Service sales
  • Automatic receipt generation
  • Multiple payment methods
  • Balance tracking on credit sales

Purchases
