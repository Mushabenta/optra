This Privacy Policy describes how Optra collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our business management application.

1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following terms have the meanings ascribed to them below:

Personal Data

Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. This includes names, email addresses, contact information, and any other data that can be used to identify you directly or indirectly.

Business Data

Information related to your business operations, including sales records, inventory data, expense tracking, staff information, and financial transactions recorded within the Optra application.

Usage Data

Information collected automatically regarding your interaction with the application, including access times, pages viewed, and the duration of your sessions.

Business Identifier

A unique identifier assigned to your business account that helps organize and segregate data between different business entities using the application.

Data Controller

You are the data controller of your business information, as all data remains under your exclusive control.

2. Information We Collect

Optra collects the following categories of information to provide and improve our business management services:

2.1 Account Information

  • Full Name: Used to personalize your account and identify users within your business
  • Email Address: Used as your primary login credential and for account recovery
  • Username: A unique identifier for your account
  • Password: Stored securely using industry-standard encryption
  • Business ID: A unique identifier that groups users belonging to the same business entity
  • Role: Your permission level within the application (Admin, Manager, Staff)

2.2 Business Information

  • Business Name: The name of your company or organization
  • Contact Information: Business phone numbers and email addresses
  • Location: Physical address of your business
  • Profile Image: Optional profile picture for your business account

2.3 Transaction Data

  • Sales Records: Customer information, items sold, quantities, prices, payment methods, and transaction dates
  • Purchase Records: Supplier information, items purchased, costs, quantities, and payment details
  • Expense Data: Expense descriptions, amounts, categories, and dates
  • Inventory Data: Stock levels, item descriptions, purchase prices, and reorder thresholds
  • Receipt Information: Transaction receipts including customer details and itemized purchases

2.4 Staff Management Data

  • Employee Information: Staff names, contact details, roles, and hire dates
  • Salary Records: Employee salaries, payment history, and compensation details
  • Attendance Data: Staff attendance records, clock-in/out times, and leave information

Note: All data collected is used solely to enhance your experience with the application. We do not track you across different websites or services.

3. How We Use Your Information

Optra uses the collected information exclusively to provide and improve our business management services. Your information is used for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Provision

  • To authenticate your identity and authorize access to the application
  • To process and record sales transactions
  • To manage inventory levels and track stock movements
  • To record and categorize business expenses
  • To generate receipts and invoices for customers
  • To calculate profit and loss statements based on sales and purchase data
  • To manage staff records and process salary payments
  • To track employee attendance and work hours

Important: Optra does not use your personal or business data for advertising, marketing, or any purposes other than those explicitly stated above. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Optra is committed to protecting your information. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

Important: Always maintain regular backups of your critical business data. We recommend exporting your data periodically for safekeeping.
